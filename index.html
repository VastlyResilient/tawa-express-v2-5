Missing

THE TREATMENT PLAN

Three Courses, Served in Order

Phase 1
Days 0–30

Complete the record

  • Write a real title tag, like: 'Tawa Express | Halal Indian-Fusion Kitchen in Bridgeport, CT' — not your web address.
  • Write a one-line meta description that reads like a chalkboard special: butter chicken tacos, garlic momos, mango lassi, on Main Street in Bridgeport.
  • Put your whole menu on the page as real text — words, not just pictures — so Google can read every dish from vada pav to hakka noodles.
Phase 2
Days 30–60

Build the pages

  • Add Restaurant schema: name, address, phone, hours, cuisine, halal, price range.
  • Add Menu markup so each dish becomes its own searchable item a phone can quote.
  • Link your Instagram and Facebook from the site, then claim and polish your Google Business Profile with real food photos and correct hours.
Phase 3
Days 60–90

Rank & compound

  • Post one fresh special a week — one photo, one line of text — so Google always smells something cooking.
  • Ask happy diners for reviews that name the dish ('the garlic momos!'). Dish words in reviews are pure SEO gold.
  • Build a small page for each of the three campuses near you: 'halal food near [school name], minutes away on Main Street.'

Phase 1 is this week. Phase 2 is this month. Phase 3 is this season. Each step is small, cheap, and permanent — like labeling jars once and cooking from them forever.
